The journey

A week on Earth, earned before the sky.

Every seat begins the same way — feet in the sand, then a flight that rewrites what you call home.

01

Arrive & ground

A villa on the Punta Cana coast. Rest, eat, and meet the five strangers who become your crew. The higher you go, the more the ground matters.

02

Train

Three days of centrifuge, cabin drills, and weightless rehearsal — taught like a sport, not a science class. By the end the launch feels familiar.

03

Launch

Dawn at Cabo Engaño. Ignition, then the long clean climb you just rode with your thumb. Eight minutes to orbit.

04

Orbit & return

Ninety minutes per lap around a planet with no borders on it. Then a gentle descent home — and you are not the same person who left.
